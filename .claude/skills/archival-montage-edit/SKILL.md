# Archival montage edit

Reference: "State Machines — enterprise apps for agents" (@statemachineshq, X, 8 Sep 2026),
1:09, 1920×1080, 23.976 fps, music ≈ 118 BPM, voice-over plus three interview voices.
Analysed at 2 fps (whole film), 8–13 fps on seven passages, plus scene-cut detection and
full-resolution stills. **The reference video is not in the repository and must not be.** We reuse
the editing grammar only. It is built from third-party archives, press photos and famous people's
interviews: for Plug every image must come from licensed, owned or generated sources (bible
SRC-01/02). Never imitate a real person or put words in their mouth.

## 1. Structure (the arc in 69 s)

| time | act | treatment |
|------|-----|-----------|
| 0:00–0:03 | **History in 3 s** | flash-frame montage, 1–4 frames per image |
| 0:04–0:06 | Thesis on black | words lit one by one: "we've been making work easier" |
| 0:06–0:17 | Three eras | one line per era as a subtitle, archive images laid on black, slow push |
| 0:17–0:23 | The question | typed monospace question over treated interview shots, 3 speakers |
| 0:23–0:31 | The stakes | scale images (data centre, rocket launch), subtitle keeps running |
| 0:32–0:35 | The quote | kinetic caption builds beside the speaker |
| 0:36–0:41 | The problem | question typed on black, then over a circular lens insert |
| 0:43–0:57 | The proof | fast press montage: listings, headlines, office, error dialogs, glitch |
| 0:57–1:09 | The answer | black screen typography: line typed, word swaps, name, logo |

Rhythm law: **alternate density and silence**. Each flash montage (≤ 0.17 s per shot) is followed by
a long, calm shot or a black screen with a few words (2–6 s). The black screens carry the
argument, the images carry the emotion.

## 2. Cuts (measured)

- **Flash-frame montage** (0:00–0:03, 0:43–0:57): 22 cuts in 3 s. Shots of 1–4 frames (40–170 ms),
  mixing engravings, paintings, B&W photos, film stills and macro shots (eyes, clocks, a brain).
  Each image is chosen to read in one glance (a single centred subject), and the series tells a
  story by itself (anatomy → time → work → offices → space). It ends on a held image (nebula,
  1 s) before the black.
- **White flash frames** (1 frame of white) between some shots, to punch the start.
- **Cut on the word**: each new image or caption lands on a stressed syllable of the voice; the
  long shots last one sentence.
- **Cuts on the music** in the fast passages (≈ 118 BPM: 2 shots per beat at the climax).
- **Hard cut to black** before every thesis line (no fade), and a short fade only at the very end.

## 3. Zooms and camera moves

- **Slow push-in** on every still (scale 1.00 → 1.06–1.12 over the shot, linear or very soft
  ease-out); no still is ever static.
- **Push on a newspaper headline** (0:13–0:15): extreme close-up of printed text with shallow depth
  of field (top and bottom lines blurred), the keyword ("AI Agents") highlighted by a marker band
  (teal, blue, yellow, red: a different paper and colour on each shot), 3–4 shots of 0.4 s each,
  each a bit closer.
- **Fish-eye / barrel** on a few archive images (Egyptian fresco), slight rotation.
- **Speed ramp into a light tunnel** (0:16–0:17): colour streaks and radial blur as a transition
  between "intelligence" and the interview section.
- **Handheld micro-shake** on interview and launch shots (a few px, low frequency).

## 4. Image treatments

- **Black & white archives on pure black**, photos as framed rectangles placed off-centre (left,
  right) rather than full frame; the subtitle stays at the bottom centre.
- **Collage building** (0:10–0:12): photo 1 appears on the right; photo 2 slides in from the left
  and overlaps it; photo 3 lands on top in the middle; each arrives on a word, with a 2-frame
  ease-out and a slight scale (1.03 → 1.00). Like prints thrown on a table.
- **Interview look**: halftone dot pattern over the face, a subtle RGB channel split (chromatic
  aberration ≈ 3–6 px), lifted blacks, warm tint, scan lines; the speaker is off-centre.
- **Glitch** (0:54): horizontal slice displacement and colour fringing for one or two frames, used
  once, on "errors".
- **Equations overlaid** on paintings (handwritten-looking maths in white) for "intelligence".

## 5. Text treatments

- **T1 Words lit one by one on black** (0:04–0:06): the whole line is laid out in dim grey,
  each word turns white as it is said, then the line breathes (tracking tightens slightly) and the
  words dim out from left to right before the next image.
- **T2 Running subtitle** at the bottom centre (small sans, white, ≈ 26 px at 1080p) carrying the
  voice-over sentence by sentence; it stays through the cuts so the montage reads as one thought.
- **T3 Typed monospace question with a block cursor** (0:17–0:23): uppercase mono, letter-spaced,
  a cyan block cursor stretches ahead of the text as a "highlight" before each word appears;
  RGB split and glow on the letters; it stays across three different speakers (the question
  links them).
- **T4 Kinetic caption beside the speaker** (0:32–0:35): bold condensed sans with glow, the quote
  stacks word by word next to the head ("THEY NEVER WILL / FORGIVE"), the key word bigger and
  italic; each line replaced by the next (no scrolling).
- **T5 Typing on black then over a lens insert** (0:36–0:41): "So how do you test software…" typed
  letter by letter; "…that can actually behave?" stays while circular microscope/lens images
  flash behind it (cells, light, rings).
- **T6 Word swap by blur** (1:02–1:05): "Work with Salesforce → ServiceNow → NetSuite": the fixed
  part stays, the variable word blurs out upward while the next blurs in from below
  (≈ 6 frames, vertical offset 20 px, blur 10 px), one swap per beat.
- **T7 Finale on black**: one line typed ("Give agents enterprise apps APIs"), keyword lit in
  yellow-green, then single words fade/blur one by one ("that work", "like", "production"), the
  name in lowercase, then the logo, held 2 s.

## 6. Sound design

Voice-over always on top; the music builds (sparse at the start, full at the proof montage,
drops out under the finale's black screens); whooshes and digital ticks on the flash montage and
the typed text; silence just before the answer.

## 7. Building it in Plug (Remotion)

- `FlashMontage`: a list of images with durations in frames (1–4) and optional white flash
  frames; durations snapped to the music beats; each image with its own micro push.
- `Still`: an image placed in a frame on black with a slow push (scale over the shot); position
  left/right/centre on the grid.
- `Collage`: photos dropped one by one on given words (word timings from Whisper).
- `HeadlinePush`: a text block rendered as print (serif, paper texture), depth-of-field blur on
  lines above/below, marker band behind the keyword, push-in.
- Treatments as CSS/SVG filters: halftone (SVG pattern mask), RGB split (three offset copies with
  `mix-blend-mode: screen`), scan lines, grain.
- Text blocks: `KaraokeLine` (src/synced/Karaoke.tsx) for T1/T5, a `TypedLine` with block cursor
  for T3/T7, `WordSwap` for T6, a kinetic stacked caption for T4 (measured, safe area).
- Images: only licensed, owned or generated material; no real people.
